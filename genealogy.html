Surname: Bates

Bates — an English patronymic surname from "Bate" (a short form of Bartholomew). This branch of the family settled in Utah in the early 20th century, with roots in Park City and the Salt Lake Valley.

The Ryan line (Beverly Opal Ryan Bates' family) joins the tree through her marriage to William Edward Bates, Jr. — Kenneth Thomas Ryan & Opal Lavon Provost Ryan of Utah.
