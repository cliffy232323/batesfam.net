Method / what the UltraRunning archive crawl did & didn't prove

Goal attempted: crawl every UltraRunning Magazine issue TOC (1984–1999), open each race-result article's public teaser.jpg (which is NOT behind the subscriber paywall — Cloudflare only gates the HTML article body and TOC pages via curl; the image files return HTTP 200 with no auth), OCR it with tesseract, and grep for "Bates."

Pipeline built & proven: TOC → article slugs → curl teaser.jpg → tesseract OCR → word-boundary grep. Ran cleanly over ~1,500 articles (1984–1988 full years + targeted Boise/UT races 1991–1993). It correctly surfaces every "Bates" occurrence (e.g. Bill Bates VA, John Bates, Charles Bates Canada — all not Gene) and zero false positives once a word-boundary regex replaced the naive substring match.

Honest limitation (discovered Aug 3, 2026): the teaser.jpg is an eco-cover / cropped collage, not the race-results page. Inspecting the Collegiate Peaks 50 Mile 1992 teaser (where Gene placed 5th, 8:12:59, a result we already hold from ultrarunning.com) showed three disjoint result columns plus an unrelated Cuyamaca 50K blurb — Gene's row was simply not in the crop. The one hit we did get (Shafer Butte 52.4 mi, June 1994, 3rd, "Gene Bates,UT 9:13") was lucky: that teaser happened to be the results table's top-3. Therefore a "no Bates" result from this crawl does NOT prove Gene didn't run that race — it only proves his name wasn't in that particular teaser crop.

Net result: the crawl is a useful negative screen but cannot confirm presence. The 6 verified results on this page stand on their primary sources (ultrarunning.com profile, Deseret News, and the Shafer Butte magazine scan). To positively backfill more Gene results from UltraRunning Magazine, the full subscriber article bodies (or scanned PDF back-issues) are required — which sit behind the login we're not using.
