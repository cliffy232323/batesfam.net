Scipio Cemetery and Scipio Pioneer Cemetery, Millard County, Utah, are the primary burial sites for the Wasden/Quarnberg line. Mountain View Memorial Estates Cemetery, Cottonwood Heights, Salt Lake County, holds the Candland line. Chester Cemetery, Sanpete County, holds the older Candland generation. Fountain Green Cemetery, Sanpete County, holds the Holman and Partridge lines. Holden Cemetery, Millard County, holds the James S. Holman line. Emery City Cemetery, Emery County, holds the Allred line. Mona Cemetery, Juab County, holds the Partridge line. Salt Lake City Cemetery holds the Maiben, Sanford Holman, and Elizabeth Allred lines.

Surnames

Wasden — Tara's paternal surname, rooted in Scipio, Utah. The earliest known member is Alma Lloyd Wasden (1911–2006); his parents are not presently recorded in available sources.

Quarnberg (also Quarnberg/Olofsson) — Swedish in origin. John Johansson Quarnberg (1821–1891) and Catherine Cecilia Olofsson (1815–1895) immigrated from Gotland, Sweden to Scipio, Utah. The surname was carried through Peter Jacob and Reuben Francis.

Hanseen — Swedish patronymic. Peter John Hanseen (1830–1920) and Christina Elizabeth Nielson (1838–1897) arrived from Gotland, Sweden and settled in Scipio. Christina's parents were Hans Nielson (1798–1871) and Anna Maria Larsdotter (1806–1877), both of Sweden.

Stone — English surname. Joseph Stone (1850–1933) was born in England and crossed the plains with Mormon pioneers in 1861, settling in Scipio.

Robins — English surname. Thomas Fullwell Robins (1824–1895) was born in Deerhurst, Gloucestershire and emigrated to Utah with his wife Ann Johnson. Buried at Scipio Pioneer Cemetery.

Johnson — English surname. William Johnson (1789–1850) and Elizabeth Ann Jauncey (1792–1883) were of Leigh, Worcestershire, England. Their daughter Ann Johnson married into the Robins line.

Candland — Darlene Candland Wasden's maiden name, of Chester, Sanpete County, Utah. Her parents were Kenneth Eugene Candland (1907–1999) and Adella Holman Candland (1910–1979). Kenneth's parents were David Harry Candland (1858–1933) and Phebe Maiben Candland (1868–1951). David Harry was a railroad station agent and farmer at Chester. The Candlands are buried at Mountain View Memorial Estates Cemetery (Kenneth & Adella) and Chester Cemetery (David Harry & Phebe).

Holman — Adella Holman Candland's maiden name. Her parents were David Lester Holman (1878–1963) and Mary LaPearl Partridge Holman (1887–1966) of Fountain Green, Sanpete County, Utah. David Lester's parents were Sanford Holman (1844–1938) and Elizabeth Hannah Allred Holman (1843–1927). Buried at Fountain Green Cemetery.

Maiben — Phebe Maiben Candland's maiden name. Her parents were Henry Maiben (1819–1883) and Flora Louise Maddison Maiben (1835–1922). Henry Maiben was director of the old Salt Lake Theater, and Phebe performed on stage as a child.

Partridge — Mary LaPearl Partridge Holman's maiden name. Her parents were John Harvey Partridge (1863–1932) and Susan Isabella Draper Partridge (1867–1946) of Goshen, Utah County, Utah. Buried at Fountain Green Cemetery.
